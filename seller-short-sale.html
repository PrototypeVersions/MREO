A short sale is different from an ordinary sale because the seller may be seeking approval for a transaction in which expected sale proceeds are insufficient to satisfy the amount owed against the property. Approval cannot be assumed.
This prototype does not determine mortgage balances, property value, deficiency liability, foreclosure status, lender approval, tax consequences, or eligibility for any loss-mitigation option. Owners should obtain appropriate legal, tax, lending, and real-estate advice for their circumstances.
