Seller Value Recovery is designed around a simple question: if rehabilitation creates additional property value, can more of that value remain with the existing owner rather than being captured only after the property is sold to another party?
Reconstruction would require property-specific scope, estimates, qualified professionals, funding arrangements, contracts, approvals, milestones, and controls. The owner should not assume that rehabilitation will increase value enough to justify its cost.
This prototype does not provide reconstruction financing, guarantee an after-repair value, engage contractors, create an escrow arrangement, or promise improved sale proceeds. All such matters would require separate review and agreement.
