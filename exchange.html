Global Sealed Bidding

Make the purchaser submit an independent offer.

Qualified purchasers can submit transaction terms without seeing competitors' offers during the sealed submission period. When that period ends, the seller can compare completed offers using the factors that matter for the particular property.

MREO Principle

Don't replace seller judgment with a score.

The scorecard should organize relevant transaction characteristics, not silently decide which buyer wins. The seller remains able to weigh the terms according to the property's actual priorities and permitted criteria.

In this example, Buyer A offers $8,000 more, while Buyer B presents stronger modeled execution characteristics.

Important Distinction

Network qualification is not settlement funding.

A participant can satisfy the requirements to enter the MREO exchange without that status automatically proving that every future property acquisition is funded. Property-specific capital, deposits, underwriting, and settlement requirements remain separately reviewable.

Backup-Bidder Waterfall

A failed buyer shouldn't automatically reset the transaction.

Where the seller's transaction rules permit it, qualified alternative purchasers can remain ordered and available for a defined period. Selecting a different bid above will also rebuild the demonstration waterfall below.

Transaction Continuity

Preserve the next executable alternative.

A backup should activate only under the applicable agreement and circumstances. Seller-caused delays, title problems, valid termination rights, or other exceptions may prevent automatic succession.

Demonstration Failure Event

Action Required

Assume the selected purchaser fails to deliver the required deposit within the contractual activation period. Instead of rebuilding marketing, qualification, and bidding from the beginning, an authorized seller could review the next still-valid backup offer.
