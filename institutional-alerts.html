The purpose of the alert layer is not merely to count problems. Each exception can remain connected to the affected property, responsible party, required action, supporting record, and deadline.

MREO's proposed Backup-Bidder Waterfall allows a transaction to preserve qualified secondary demand. If a selected purchaser misses a required performance deadline, an eligible backup purchaser may be activated according to the seller's governing transaction rules rather than restarting the entire marketing process.
