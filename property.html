Source-aware valuation

Don't hide where the number came from.

Automated valuations, broker opinions, appraisals, reconstruction estimates, public records, and projected repaired values perform different functions. MREO can preserve the origin and purpose of each rather than silently collapsing them into one number.

Record Value Source Type Updated Integrity / Status
VT

MREO does not need to claim that one automated estimate is “the value.” A property can instead contain several attributed values whose source, date, function, assumptions, and reliability remain visible to the people making decisions.

Property Outcome Analysis

What should happen to this property?

Instead of assuming every property should immediately become a conventional listing, MREO can compare alternative pathways around the owner's actual objective, expected value, costs, time, uncertainty, and property condition.

All pathway figures shown in this prototype are illustrative models. They are not appraisals, offers, financial projections, financing commitments, or guarantees of actual proceeds.

Qualified demand

Views are not buyers.

MREO can distinguish casual marketplace attention from purchasers who have completed defined qualification requirements. When appropriate, offers can be compared by execution strength rather than headline price alone.
