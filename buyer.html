Before the Offer

Separate property readiness from buyer readiness.

A property may be ready for acquisition while a buyer is not. Likewise, a highly qualified buyer may be evaluating an asset that still requires title correction, field verification, professional review, or reconstruction analysis.

Backup-Bidder Waterfall

Losing the bid doesn't have to end the opportunity.

Qualified backup purchasers can agree to remain available for a defined period. If the selected buyer fails a material activation requirement, the seller can potentially move to the next approved offer without reopening the entire market.
