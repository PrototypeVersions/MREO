Current visual evidence

Field evidence can provide current visual information about a property while preserving the source and status of the record.

The primary property photograph is connected to the same persistent MREO property record used throughout the prototype. The main image shown here therefore matches the image shown on Browse Properties, the main property page, and the Visual Record.

Remote Verification

Visual evidence does not replace professional inspection.

Current photographs, geotagged video, construction documentation, and authorized walkthroughs can help a remote participant understand present property conditions. Professional interpretation may still require an inspector, engineer, contractor, appraiser, or other qualified participant.
