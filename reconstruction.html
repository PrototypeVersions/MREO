Controlled Draw Structure

Fund the work in stages.

Rather than releasing an entire reconstruction budget at once, funding can be organized around approved scopes, completed milestones, evidence, professional review, and explicit authorization.

01

Scope Approved

Define the work, exclusions, materials, permits, schedule, contingencies, and contractor responsibility.

02

Funds Reserved

Approved reconstruction capital is associated with the project but not treated as freely released cash.

03

Work Performed

Contractor completes the authorized milestone according to the approved reconstruction scope.

04

Evidence Added

Photographs, field observations, invoices, permits, inspection information, and other required evidence become part of the property record.

05

Approval

The authorized party determines whether the milestone satisfies the applicable release requirements.

06

Draw Released

The applicable financing or escrow provider can then process payment according to the governing agreement.

Change Control

Don't let scope creep become invisible.

New conditions are common in reconstruction. A change order can remain distinct from the original budget, show its economic impact, require explicit approval, and preserve the supporting reason and competing alternatives.

CO

A $6,800 change order is not merely another line in a contractor invoice. It changes the economics of the property. MREO can show how an added construction cost affects the remaining budget, financing needs, modeled seller proceeds, investor return, and expected value recovery.
