Missing information stays visible.

A property does not become “ready” simply because it has entered the portfolio. Missing records, unresolved title information, stale verification, and other deficiencies can remain attached to the property's persistent record until resolved.

MREO's proposed Property Passport model preserves the source and status of property information as the record evolves. Missing information and known defects are not intended to disappear simply because a property advances to another stage.
