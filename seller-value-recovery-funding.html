A reconstruction-capital structure can separate committed funds from released funds. Money can remain controlled until agreed work, documentation, and approval conditions have been met.
MREO's proposed role is coordination rather than blind automatic release of money. In a real implementation, regulated financial, escrow, settlement, lending, or other authorized institutions would hold or transfer funds where required.
This prototype does not offer financing, solicit an investment, hold money, create an escrow account, approve contractors, authorize payments, or guarantee that reconstruction capital will be available.
