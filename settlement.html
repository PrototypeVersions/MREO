Programmable Settlement

Automate workflow without blindly automating authority.

MREO can automatically monitor deadlines, required records, approvals, and dependencies. That does not mean a single software field should automatically release money, transfer title, or substitute for professional judgment.

01

Detect

Identify that an expected deadline, document, approval, deposit, or transaction condition has not been satisfied.

02

Notify

Alert the appropriate authorized participant rather than silently allowing a deadline to pass.

03

Prepare

Draft an appropriate status request, checklist, reminder, or workflow action for review.

04

Verify

Confirm that the required condition has actually been completed and evidenced.

05

Authorize

Preserve the applicable human, professional, contractual, financial, or legal approval requirement.

06

Record

Update the Property Passport with the completed settlement state and its source.

Policy-Governed Automation

“Condition satisfied” should mean more than a checkbox.

A programmable transaction layer can evaluate whether required records exist and whether deadlines have passed, but legal or financial consequences should follow the governing agreement and authorized professional process.
