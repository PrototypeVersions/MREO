Coordinate the people needed to move a property forward.

Coordination

MREO connects roles without collapsing them.

Inspectors remain inspectors. Contractors remain contractors. Settlement providers remain settlement providers. MREO's proposed role is to coordinate the work, records, deadlines, permissions, and property status connecting those participants.

Shared Property Record

Each participant sees the part they need.

A contractor may need the approved scope and milestone requirements. A settlement provider may need title and transaction documents. A field professional may need access authorization and verification instructions. They do not all need unrestricted access to every record connected to the property.

A full implementation should identify each participant's role, authority, compensation, required license or qualification, and relationship to the owner or purchaser. MREO coordination should not obscure who is actually providing a service.
