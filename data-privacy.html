Protocol · Data & Privacy

Data & Privacy

Keep the property record connected without giving every participant access to everything.

01 · Access

Role-Based Access

Access can depend on the participant's role and the task being performed. A contractor, purchaser, settlement provider, property manager, and owner may each need different information.

02 · Protection

Protect Sensitive Records

Sensitive documents do not need to be publicly exposed simply because the property has a persistent digital record. Private records can remain protected, encrypted, or stored outside any public ledger while their status remains connected to the property workflow.

03 · Minimum Necessary Data

Share Only What Is Needed

Participants and automated systems should receive only the information and authority needed for their approved task. Access can be narrowed rather than treating the property record as one unrestricted database.

Example

Contractor Approved scope and milestone requirements
Purchaser Authorized property and transaction information
Settlement Provider Title, funds, documents, and closing requirements
AI Assistant Only data and authority required for its approved task

Property Passport

Connected does not mean public.

A Property Passport can preserve the existence, source, status, and lifecycle of relevant records while access to the underlying information remains controlled according to role, permission, and legal requirements.

A future MREO implementation could use hashes, digital signatures, or other verification methods to help establish that a record has not been altered without placing the sensitive document itself on a public blockchain or ledger.
