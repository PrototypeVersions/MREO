Operating Principle

The protocol connects the property lifecycle.

MREO is designed to keep property records, participants, permissions, decisions, transaction activity, reconstruction, and operations connected rather than separating each stage into unrelated systems.

MREO is presented here as a proposed protocol and demonstration prototype. Features described throughout the site should not be interpreted as currently deployed, licensed, funded, or available services unless expressly identified otherwise.
