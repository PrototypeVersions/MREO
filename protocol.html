The Protocol

The transaction is only one part of the property lifecycle.

MREO

Keep the property record connected.

MREO is designed to coordinate the people, information, permissions, deadlines, and property activity that may otherwise become separated across the real-estate lifecycle.

01
Professional Network

Coordinate local work.

Inspections, reconstruction, field verification, settlement, property operations, and other services.

→ 02
Data & Privacy

Control who can see what.

Property records can remain connected without exposing every document or sensitive detail to every participant.

→ 03
AI & Technology

Assist the workflow.

Use software and authorized AI assistance to organize, compare, monitor, and prepare information for human action.

→

Persistent Asset Identity

One property. Many stages.

Intake, evaluation, bidding, contract, settlement, reconstruction, financing, operation, and resale can contribute to one evolving property record rather than creating disconnected information at every stage.

MREO is intended as coordination infrastructure. The protocol does not eliminate the need for licensed professionals, regulated institutions, legal agreements, inspections, appraisals, title work, or other services required by a particular property or transaction.
This website demonstrates a proposed MREO architecture. It does not represent a fully implemented, licensed, funded, or operational global real-estate protocol.
