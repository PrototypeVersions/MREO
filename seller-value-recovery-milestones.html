If reconstruction conditions change, revised work and cost should be recorded as a change order rather than silently replacing the original scope or budget.
A full MREO reconstruction ledger could preserve approved scope, estimates, committed capital, milestone evidence, change orders, payments, and remaining budget as the property's condition changes over time.
This prototype does not verify construction work, approve invoices, inspect completed repairs, hold capital, or release funds. Actual reconstruction and payment controls would depend on contracts, qualified professionals, authorized financial institutions, and applicable law.
